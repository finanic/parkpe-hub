              Economics · TDS

              TDS

              Section 194H for every retailer, 206AA when PAN is missing, and a 197 certificate only for the retailer who holds one.

              TDS policy

              Applies when commission is credited to retailers.

              194H is the rate for a retailer whose PAN is on file. 206AA applies only when that account has no PAN. TAN is the company’s, once for every retailer.

              Lower deduction requests

              Retailers file Section 197 on their dashboard. Approve one retailer. Past credits inside the dates are repriced and the extra TDS is credited back.
